# APRENDO GitHub Repository  
Welcome to **APRENDO's GitHub repository**, a hub for open-source blockchain educational initiatives across Latin America. We believe in leveraging **blockchain-based solutions** to foster **financial inclusion** and **digital empowerment** in underserved communities.  


## 🚀 APRENDOlab: Hands-on smart contract solutions for local challenges. 
**APRENDOlab** is our current program hosted in Github. A hands-on workshops where non-coders participants from communities across Latam collaborate with the APRENDO developer team to design and test a Proof of Concept dApp, exploring how local challenges could be tackled using Ethereum.


### 🎯 2025 Objectives for APRENDOlab:
#APRENDOlab focuses on empowering individuals and communities while showcasing Ethereum's transformative potential in local contexts.

-Test Ethereum Use Cases in Local Latin American vulnerable Communities:  Facilitate the experimentation and testing of Ethereum-based solutions to address specific local needs, such as financial inclusion, governance, or resource allocation. 
-Enhance Practical Knowledge: Provide hands-on experience in designing and testing decentralized applications (dApps) for real-world use cases using Ethereum.
-Empower Communities: Equip participants with advanced blockchain skills tailored to their local challenges, enabling them to explore and locate meaningful solutions that resonate with their unique socio-economic contexts.

## 🛠️ Open-Source Projects  

We are committed to **building in public** and ensuring that our work contributes to the **Ethereum community as a public good**. Our repository will include:  
✅ **Smart contracts** from APRENDOlab PoCs  
✅ **Educational resources** used in our #APRENDOcripto and #APRENDO+ programs  
✅ **Impact reports & research findings** from #APRENDOexplora and #APRENDOlab 

## 📚 About APRENDO  

APRENDO is a **nonprofit organization** dedicated to **blockchain education and social impact**. Since 2021, we have:  
- Trained **1,264 participants** through 69 editions of #APRENDOcripto across **10 countries**.  
- Developed **advanced blockchain learning programs** with NGOs and tech education centers.  
- Led the **Social Impact vertical** of Argentina’s **Crecimiento movement**, positioning the country as a key hub for blockchain adoption.
